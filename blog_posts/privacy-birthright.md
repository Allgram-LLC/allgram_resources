Have you ever seen an online ad for something you were just talking about?

Those digital déjà-vus are kind of scary, right? The thing is, they’re a feature of social media, not a bug.

So, are they listening to us?

In 2016, Facebook [published](https://about.fb.com/news/h/facebook-does-not-use-your-phones-microphone-for-ads-or-news-feed-stories/) a blog post stating, “Facebook does not use your phone’s microphone to inform ads or to change what you see in News Feed.” In 2018, Mark Zuckerberg [testified](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pTvF8Docgk0) before the Senate that our mobiles aren’t listening. In 2025, Instagram CEO Adam Mosseri [denied](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DPRA3qyEgWw/?hl=en) that Instagram actively listens, with Facebook’s terms and conditions [saying](https://www.facebook.com/privacy/dialog/is-facebook-listening-to-my-conversation) much the same.

Mosseri’s statement, however, came just as Meta [announced](https://www.forbes.com/sites/kateoflahertyuk/2025/10/06/meta-ai-confirms-your-data-will-be-used-for-ads-heres-how-and-when/) that—for Facebook, Instagram and Threads users—data from your interactions with their AI will help [push](https://about.fb.com/news/2025/10/improving-your-recommendations-apps-ai-meta/) more ads your way. Lucky you.

Thanks to those algos, they don’t even need to listen to our conversations.

## Social media is social surveillance

With centralized social media, your data isn't yours—it's theirs—hoarded on corporate servers, sold to advertisers and stolen by hackers. It invites disaster, always one exploit away from your privacy being stolen.

Don’t forget the Instagram [hack](https://cybersecuritynews.com/instagram-data-leak-exposes-sensitive-info-of-17-5m-accounts/) in 2025, Twitter [2022](https://www.malwarebytes.com/blog/news/2022/08/twitter-confirmed-july-2022-data-breach-affecting-5-4m-users), [LinkedIn](https://www.forbes.com/sites/leemathews/2021/06/29/details-on-700-million-linkedin-users-for-sale-on-notorious-hacking-forum/) and [Facebook](https://www.wired.com/story/facebook-data-leak-500-million-users-phone-numbers/) in 2021, [Sina Weibo](https://wolfesystems.com.au/the-digital-frontier-unravelling-the-2020-sina-weibo-breach/) 2020, Yahoo [2013](https://www.huntress.com/threat-library/data-breach/yahoo-data-breach) and [2014](https://digitalcommons.wcl.american.edu/aulr/vol66/iss5/3/), [MySpace](https://www.huntress.com/threat-library/data-breach/myspace-data-breach), [Tumblr](https://www.sgllp.co.uk/insurance-solution/three-years-after-a-major-security-breach-at-tumblr-65m-passwords-and-emails-for-sale/), and [VK](https://www.cyberdaily.au/security/11065-390-million-users-impacted-by-alleged-russian-messaging-app-data-breach-as-vk-denies-any-hack)—billions compromised by centralized data vaults that put us all at risk.

In a world of infinite scrolls, those ads echoing your private chats aren't coincidence, they're surveillance—and you’re the product, you’re the merch.
